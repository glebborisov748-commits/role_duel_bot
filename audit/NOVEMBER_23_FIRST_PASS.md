# November 23 — first repair pass

This implements the agreed smaller first pass. It does not claim the larger game redesign or photo-to-character creation is complete.

## Delivered

- Online rooms: explicit in-page room preview and join confirmation, code or invitation-link input, persistent age consent/skin/stake while inspecting a room, larger 18+ confirmation and visible errors. Tested with two real signed test accounts in all five games and with the Telegram bridge inside the app iframe.
- Recovery: dead accounts can read app state and access a recovery panel, use a stocked defibrillator, buy revival or reset into creation. Ordinary chat/game mutations and unrelated invoices stay blocked. Revival restores needs to avoid immediately dying again; creating a new companion clears the relationship but keeps purchases/subscription.
- Interactive onboarding: input, send, energy indicator, Shop, Work, Wardrobe and Profile advance after the requested action, not a sequence of Next buttons. Skip and replay remain available. Uses existing generated pointer art and does not spend currency during the navigation steps; sending is clearly a real message.
- Energizer: original square image proportions, slightly lower placement, 0.35-second resistant pull after shaking. Guidance waits 2.5 seconds, appears for one second per cycle and stops on interaction. Phone-motion hint is shown only when the motion listener is enabled. Existing sound/opened art retained.
- Message effect: slightly higher drift, full input width on desktop, final opacity held after animation so the copy cannot flash back. A small white eye acknowledges the send before typing; no artificial delay of an actual reply. Avatar tap opens its image; balance plus opens currency purchases.
- Channel: named profile card using the configured channel handle, plus a small optional chat banner. The configured channel currently has no dedicated supplied icon, so the existing chat icon is used.
- Bucks offers: one shared seeded random product per fixed two-day period, purchasable once per account during that period. Personal 72-hour product timers are retired. The active item and time to rotation are visible; stale requests cannot buy a different item. Existing prices retained pending a broader economy review.
- Wardrobe: no cabinet doors/cross, owned/shop/category filters, collapsed slot controls, and only owned prepared combinations offered for selection. A one-time simple T-shirt/cap starter grant preserves existing inventory. Base female clothes are a white tank and shorts; base male clothes are a white T-shirt and shorts. Equipped slots replace the corresponding base description. New whole mannequin art covers the base, single T-shirt/denim-short examples and replacement police outfits. Arbitrary combinations and some small garments still use registered layers and need the later expanded whole-look art pass; this is not an exhaustive render of all combinations.
- Police: existing purchased police item keys now point to fitted opaque glossy latex uniforms; no duplicate purchase is required. Image facts include a modest harness.
- Toys: a separate preview-only 18+ catalog, with age/family-friendly and relationship-level-9 gates enforced on the server. Seven product descriptions; no purchases, photo facts or equip actions. The generator rejected the requested combined toy-image sheet; new recognizable toy artwork, including the vibrator card, is not delivered. No alternate explicit imagery was substituted.
- Species: simpler visible choices hide legacy android/animatronic/duplicate dragon entries, while existing saved characters still load. Furry choices use plain names. Missing character names use gender-appropriate pronouns. Solo strip-game instructions focus on non-graphic clothing reactions, without injecting the round's rock/paper/scissors symbols. No model or provider was changed.
- Wires: both variants now use dragging to connect, by color or by shape; no blocker-clearing trace puzzle.
- Admin: role-specific /admin_help includes the existing chief-only /giveenergizers command. /tehwork preview on/off toggles the maintenance mini-game only for the chief, without changing the global maintenance setting.
- Separate staging configuration: dedicated bot token/domain/storage, tester allowlist, Telegram invoice guard, sample environment and deployment instructions. No second bot or hosting service was provisioned; credentials and a separate host deployment are still required. Production remains the default configuration.

## Validation

197 unit tests; signed billing/upgrade/entitlement/refund runtime checks; two-account browser room flows in five games; recovery and level-9 catalog gates; mobile 320/390/430px tutorial and energy interaction; desktop outgoing-effect geometry/final opacity; embedded online authentication. Telegram, language and app photo providers were mocked. Separate asset-generation calls produced the new clothing illustrations. No real Stars transaction or live app-provider generation was used in validation.

## Economy observation

This is an upper-bound/source review, not observed player telemetry. With current nominal free hacker pay of 110 bucks, a 12,000-buck energizer costs at least 110 successful hacks; two photos for 18,000 cost 164. Penalties, care spending and human play time make actual acquisition slower. The server's four-second payout interval is only an anti-spam floor, not a prediction of human play speed. The shared item limit means at most one exchange purchase in each two-day period, regardless of farm wealth. Two daily quests can still award energy/scenes and the wheel remains probabilistic, so recurring giveaway volume should be measured before tightening rewards. Subscription photo grants remain 45/90/200 monthly or 10/20/45 weekly. These figures alone do not establish revenue or retention balance.
